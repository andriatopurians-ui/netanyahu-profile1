PROFILE


Born in Tel Aviv in 1949. Army officer, diplomat and leader of the Likud party, he has served as Prime Minister of Israel in three separate periods, the longest cumulative tenure in the country's history.


Raised in Jerusalem and the United States, Benjamin Netanyahu served in the Israel Defense Forces' Sayeret Matkal commando unit. He studied architecture and management at MIT, then moved into diplomacy in the 1980s. He has led the Likud party since 1993 and, in 1996, became Israel's youngest prime minister and the first born after the state's founding.

Record

Four decades in Israeli public life. Supporters and critics disagree sharply about his record.

Diplomat

Deputy chief of mission in Washington, then Israel's ambassador to the United Nations, 1984–1988.

Party leader

Elected chairman of Likud in 1993 and has led the party for most of the time since.

Prime minister

Served 1996–1999, 2009–2021, and again from December 2022.

Legal cases

Indicted in 2019 on corruption charges, which he denies. The ICC issued an arrest warrant in 2024, which Israel contests.
